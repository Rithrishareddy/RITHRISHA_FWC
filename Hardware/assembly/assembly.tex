\textbf{Q.8} \\
The minimal sum-of-products expression for the logic function $f$ represented by the given Karnaugh map is

\bigskip

\noindent
\textbf{K-map:}

\begin{center}
    \includegraphics[width=0.5\textwidth]{kmap.png}
\end{center}

\bigskip

\noindent
(A) $QS + PR\overline{S} + PQ\overline{R} + PRS + PQR$ \\
(B) $\overline{Q}S + PRS + PQ\overline{R} + PRS + PQR$ \\
(C) $PR\overline{S} + P\overline{Q}R + PRS + PQ\overline{R}$ \\
(D) $\overline{P}RS + PQ\overline{R} + PRS + P\overline{Q}R$ \\

\bigskip

\noindent
\textbf{Answer:} (A)

\bigskip

\noindent
\textbf{Explanation:} \\
Analyzing the given Karnaugh map:
\begin{itemize}
    \item We identify adjacent 1s and group them into the largest possible clusters (octets, quads, pairs).
    \item The largest groups formed are:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item A group covering RS = 01 with PQ = 00, 01, 11 $\rightarrow QS$.
        \item A group covering PQ = 01, RS = 11, 10 $\rightarrow PR\overline{S}$.
        \item A group covering PQ = 01 and 11 with RS = 01 $\rightarrow PQ\overline{R}$.
        \item A group covering PQ = 11, RS = 01, 11 $\rightarrow PRS$.
        \item A group covering PQ = 11 with RS = 01 $\rightarrow PQR$.
    \end{itemize}
    \item Combining these terms gives:
    \[
    QS + PR\overline{S} + PQ\overline{R} + PRS + PQR
    \]
    which matches option (A).
\end{itemize}
